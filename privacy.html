# Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 8, 2026 ## 1. Overview This policy describes how TikTok Uploader handles information when used by its operator. TikTok Uploader is a personal Python tool that runs locally on the operator's computer to upload videos to TikTok using TikTok's official developer API. ## 2. Information Processed Depending on its configuration, the tool may access or process: * Video files prepared for upload. * Video metadata, including titles, descriptions, hashtags, and tags. * A TikTok access token used to authorize API requests. * Upload identifiers and status information returned by TikTok. ## 3. Local Storage Video files, metadata, credentials, and upload records may be stored locally on the operator's computer. The tool's source code repository should not contain access tokens or other authentication secrets. ## 4. TikTok and Third-Party Services When a video is uploaded, relevant content and information are transmitted to TikTok. TikTok processes that information under its own privacy policy and applicable terms. This tool does not independently operate a public user account system. ## 5. Sharing and Sale of Information The tool is designed for personal use and does not intentionally sell personal information or share it with third parties for their independent marketing purposes. Information is transmitted to TikTok as needed to perform authorized uploads. ## 6. Data Retention and Security Local files and upload records may remain on the operator's computer until deleted. Authentication tokens should be kept private and excluded from public repositories. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. Children's Privacy This tool is not designed to collect personal information from children through a public-facing service. ## 8. Policy Changes This policy may be updated if the tool's functionality or information-handling practices change. ## 9. Contact For questions about this policy, contact: benjaminbuissness@gmail.com.
